### Personal Reflections

#### What the team did well
The team had very good communcation all around and a great team spirit. We took quick and good decisions and put full trust in eachother and our ideas and implementations. Every idea got a fair shot and we made sure to have fun aswell.

#### Improvement opportunities for the team
As a team we could have had more structure around the agile process and ticket workflow. We found ourselves sometimes coming up with tickets on the go and even if it worked very well for us it can be a good to be more serious about that to prepeare for the jobs we will get in the future. 

#### What I did well
I am really proud of the weather system even though that was in blueprints i really liked to play around with the niagara particle system and to get a massive visual effect looking good and i think i brought some good debugging help when it came to compiler errors or in general C++ implementations. 

I belive i also learned a lot of the git commands and felt more and more confident in using git and version control from the command line interface. 

#### Improvement opportunities for me
I found myself less motivated during the final week of the project due to some fatigue that was probably caused due to too little rest. I was balancing working part time during the weekend with the full time studies and i had no problem with getting the job done and contributing but it was tough for my mental sometimes. 

For the next project i will take on less work during the weekends to give myself time to rest and reload.
